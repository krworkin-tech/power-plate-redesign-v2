    Questions

    Good questions, honest answers.

    Does vibration training really work?

    Yes. Power Plate is used by world-class athletes like Rafael Nadal, F1 drivers, PGA golfers and NFL players, and by everyday people. Hundreds of studies support whole body vibration training, and independent studies have shown Power Plate activates up to 138% more muscle fibers compared to conventional exercise.

    Is Power Plate just for athletes?

    No, it's designed for everyone. Power Plate is used by people in their 70s looking for a light 10-minute session and by world champions who need intense training. Whether your goal is muscle strength or bone density, everyone can benefit.

    Do the vibrations hurt?

    No. The vibrations aren't overpowering or aggressive. They're designed to support your training without being uncomfortable or painful.

    Is Power Plate loud?

    No. Power Plate platforms are remarkably quiet and designed for use at home.
